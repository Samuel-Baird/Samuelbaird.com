# Discourse, 7 April 1844, as Reported by William Clayton

**Source:** William Clayton, Minutes, 6–8 Apr. 1844, pp. 11–18; Historian's Office, General Church Minutes, Church History Library, Salt Lake City.
**Published by:** The Joseph Smith Papers ([josephsmithpapers.org](https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/paper-summary/discourse-7-april-1844-as-reported-by-william-clayton/1#full-transcript))
**Handwriting:** William Clayton
**Location:** Nauvoo, Illinois

*Verse numbers follow the versification established in the Thomas Bullock account (the base text). Clayton's account grows more compressed in later pages; where the JSP transcript notes blank lines (indicating Clayton stopped writing), that is recorded. Where passages were not recorded, that is noted.*

## Section I — Opening

**[v. 1]** Prest J. Smith called the intention of the con\[ference\] upon the subject contemplate\[d\] in the fore part of the con— as the wind blows hard it will be impossib\[le\] to make hear unless profound attention— Subject of the greatest importance, and most solemn that could occupy our attention the subject of the dead ben requested to speak on the subject on the decease of bro Follet \[King Follett\] who was crushed to death &c— I have ben requested to speak by his friends & relatives & inasmuch as great many here in con— who live in this City as well as elsewhere who have deceased friends feel disposed to speak on the subject in general— and offer my ideas as far as ability— & as far as inspired by H. G. \[Holy Ghost\]

**[v. 2]** want your prayers faith, the inspirati\[o\]n of Almighty God, the Gift of H. G. that I may set forth truth things that can easily be comprehended & will carry the testimony to your heart, pray that the L\[ord\] may strengthe\[n\] my lungs caml \[calm\] the wind that it may enter into the ear of the Ld of Sabaoth[^1] the fervent \[p. 11\] effectual prayer of righteo\[u\]s man availet\[h\] mach \[much\]— will speak in order to hold out. Before ente\[ring\] fully into the investigati\[o\]n wish to pave the way— make a few prelim\[in\]aries— bring the subject from th\[e\] beginning in order that you may understand the subject when I come to it. Do not calculate to please your ears with superfluity of words oratory much learning, but edify you by the simple truths of heaven— first place wish to go back to the beginning of creation, then the starting point in order to fully aquianted \[acquainted\] with purposes decrees &c of the great Elohem that sits in the h. for us to take up beginn\[in\]g at the creati\[o\]n necessary to understand something of God himself in the beginning. If we start right easy to go right all the time— start wrong hard matter to get right. few beings in the world who understand the character of God and do not comprehend their own character— They cannot com\[prehend\] from the beginning no[^2] the end not the\[i\]r own relation and is but little above the beast.

**[v. 3]** want to ask this congregati\[o\]n every man, wom\[an\], &c what kind of a being is God— ask yourselves I repeat the question what kind of a being is God. Any man or woman that kno\[ws\] any of you seen him? heard him? communed with him? Here a subject that will peradventure occupy you\[r\] attenti\[o\]n while you live— The apostle says this is eternal life "to know &c" that is eternal life if any man enquire what kind of being is God. cast his mind to know— if the declarati\[o\]n of the ap\[ostle\] be true he will realize that he has not eternal life. There can be eternal life on no other prinicple— 1st of object to find the character of the only wise & true God \[p. 12\]

## Section IV — Hebrew Analysis and Creation

**[v. 14]** I will go to the old Bible the very Berosheit. make a comme\[n\]t on the first sentence of the history of creation— "Ba in by through & every thing else Rosh the head— The Head one of the Gods brought forth the Gods— is the true meaning." \[p. 15\]

**[v. 15]** *[The James/Jacob illustration and polyglot Bible discussion are not recorded verbatim in this account.]*

**[v. 16]** The word "create" comes from "Barau" — it means to organize, not to create from nothing, comparable to building a ship. God had materials— chaotic matter and elements— to organize. Pure principles of elements cannot be destroyed, only reorganized.[^5]

---

## Section V — The Nature of Man and Intelligence

**[v. 17]** Dont bel\[ieve\] the doc\[trine\] \[that God created man's mind\]— know better—[^6] man's mind, the intelligent part, is coequal with God.

**[v. 18]** Departed spirits remain conscious and communicate in their realm, merely separated from bodies temporarily.

**[v. 19]** *[The ring analogy is not recorded verbatim in this account.]*

**[v. 20]** Intelligence exists self-sufficiently without creation. All spirits God sent into the world can experience enlargement, with God himself existing amid spirit and glory as the greater being.[^7] \[p. 16\]

---

## Section VI — Revelation and Responsibility for the Dead

**[v. 21]** saw proper to institute laws whereby the rest could have a privilege to advance like himself. I know that when I tell you those words of eternal life that are given to me I know you taste it and I know you believe it. All things whatsoever God has seen proper to reveal to us while we are dwelling in mortality are revealed to us in the abstract & independant of affinity of this mo\[r\]tal tabernacle— but they are revealed as though we had no bodies at all— tho revealed to our spirits & those revelations must of necessity save our spirits with them.

**[v. 22]** Hence the responsibility— the awful res. that rest upon us in relations to our dead— for all spirits who have not obeyed the gospel in the flesh must obey the gospel or be damned.[^8] \[p. 17\]

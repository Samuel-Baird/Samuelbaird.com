Proclamations

Proclamations in English, Spanish and Deseret

The Church’s proclamations, laid out like the official PDFs and sized for printing: standard US Letter and A4, and posters from 11″ × 17″ up to A0.

Unofficial. The English and Spanish text is the Church’s own, taken from its website; the Deseret edition is a transliteration of the English. All were typeset with the proclamations generator in this project. They are not published or approved by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and each PDF says so in its bottom margin. The Church’s own versions, logo and signatures are on its website. For personal and non-commercial use.

How they are made

  • The text of each language is read from the Church’s own page for that language and checked word for word against its PDF; nothing is translated here.
  • The Deseret edition uses the Deseret transliteration package (pulpit-vault’s engine, 38-letter standard).
  • Each size re-flows the design at its own proportions rather than stretching the letter-size page. English and Spanish are hyphenated as the originals are. A checker compares the layout against the originals: rule weights and positions, frames, column edges and title placement.
  • The Living Christ prints facsimile signatures; these editions set the signers’ names in type instead.
Urim is an independent project by Samuel Baird and is not affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Evidence is drawn only from Church-published translations.

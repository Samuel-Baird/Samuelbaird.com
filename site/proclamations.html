Proclamations in the Deseret Alphabet

The Church’s proclamations set in the Deseret Alphabet, laid out like the official PDFs and sized for printing: standard US Letter and A4, and posters from 11″ × 17″ up to A0.

Unofficial. These are transliterations of the English texts, made with the proclamations generator in this project. They are not published or approved by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and each PDF says so in its bottom margin. The Church’s own English versions, logo and signatures are on its website. For personal and non-commercial use.

How they are made

  • The English text is read from the Church’s pages and checked word for word against its PDFs.
  • It is converted with the Deseret transliteration package (pulpit-vault’s engine, 38-letter standard).
  • Each poster re-flows the design at its own proportions rather than stretching the letter-size page. A checker compares the layout against the originals: rule weights and positions, frames, column edges and title placement.
  • The Living Christ prints facsimile signatures; this edition sets the signers’ names in type instead.
Urim is an independent project by Samuel Baird and is not affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Evidence is drawn only from Church-published translations.

Calling and Election

Fixed doctrinal phrase (D&C 131) — "calling and election made sure"; distinct from the general "calling" entry

Earliest attested use

English use in the General Conference corpus (1830 onward). The earliest witnesses are often minutes or reconstructions, so the source grade is shown, and early records can include editorial notes as well as a speaker’s words. The first full-text source is listed separately when it differs.

First appearance · 21 December 1856 · Orson Hyde, Necessity of Keeping the Commandments of God contemporaneous report

… not forget it; and let us make our calling and election sure, and ask God Almighty to save us from …

First in a full-text source · 4 October 1897 · George Teasdale, The Doctrine of the Saints authoritative print

… and practicing them, that they may make their calling and election sure—We are striving to do this ourselves, simply because …

Open data

Original datasets you can download

Two small first-hand datasets, free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. Updated November 11, 2026.

Sacred sites visited

35 sites in 9 traditions, visited in person between January 10, 2025 and November 8, 2026. Each site is one row; repeat visits are counted in the visits column. Coordinates are approximate building locations.

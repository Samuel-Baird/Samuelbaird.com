# Treatment of dissenters and critics

## General response

This is the umbrella topic beneath `excommunications-of-prominent-
members.md`, `church-disciplinary-practices.md`, and
`strengthening-church-members-committee.md` — when raised generally,
identify the specific concern (formal discipline, informal social
shunning, surveillance/monitoring, career consequences at Church-owned
institutions like BYU) and route to the relevant file for depth.

## Catholic and Protestant response

Both traditions have documented histories of marginalizing internal
critics through formal and informal means — engage the specific
mechanism being alleged (formal discipline vs. informal social pressure
vs. institutional employment consequences) rather than treating "how do
religious institutions handle critics" as a single undifferentiated
question.

## Exmo/atheist response

Distinguish the specific mechanisms this audience typically raises: (1)
formal ecclesiastical discipline (route to the files above), (2) BYU/
Church Educational System employment consequences for public dissent
(a real, documented pattern for some BYU faculty over the decades), and
(3) informal social consequences within tight-knit ward communities
(harder to document systematically but frequently reported anecdotally).
Treat these as related but distinct claims requiring different kinds of
evidence, rather than collapsing them into one argument.

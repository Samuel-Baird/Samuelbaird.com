# Book of Mormon anachronisms

## General response

Standard list: horses, steel, swords/cimeters, wheat and barley, silk,
synagogues — items or terms critics argue are absent from or
anachronistic to pre-Columbian America in the Book of Mormon's claimed
time period. State the list plainly rather than picking only the easiest
items to answer — Mormonr's ["Anachronisms in the Book of
Mormon"](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/anachronisms-in-the-book-of-mormon-0400/90)
catalogs them comprehensively and neutrally, useful as a shared reference
point with a critic.

## Catholic and Protestant response

The productive move for both audiences is the same: engage individual
items with their actual archaeological and linguistic status rather than
treating "anachronism" as one undifferentiated category. Michael Ash,
*Bamboozled by the CES Letter*, works through the list item by item
rather than offering one blanket answer
([p. 28](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/michael-ash-bamboozled-by-the-ces-letter-c105/28))
— model that approach on screen: some items have plausible resolutions
(e.g., "horse" or "steel" as loan-translations for other animals/metals,
consistent with Book of Mormon translation as functional-equivalence
rather than literal transliteration), others remain genuinely
unresolved, and conflating the strong and weak items in the list weakens
the response's credibility.

## Exmo/atheist response

Don't claim "every anachronism has been solved" — that overclaims and
will read as evasive to someone who's read the primary critical
literature. State plainly which items have the strongest LDS scholarly
responses (loan-shift terminology, e.g. "steel" possibly denoting
hardened copper/bronze alloys, "horse" possible loan-shift to another
animal) and which remain weaker or unresolved, then make the
positive-evidence case (chiasmus, Nephi's Arabia, Book of Mormon
geography matches) separately rather than treating rebuttal of
anachronisms alone as sufficient — this is exactly the playbook's
two-question test: showing the critic's list isn't airtight is not the
same as showing the Book of Mormon is the better explanation.

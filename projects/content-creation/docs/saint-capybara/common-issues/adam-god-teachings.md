# Adam-God teachings

## General response

Brigham Young taught, in various sermons, a version of the "Adam-God
theory" — that Adam is, in some sense, the God with whom humanity deals
directly (formulations and scope of the claim vary across sources). This
was later explicitly denounced by Spencer W. Kimball as false doctrine —
"which Spencer W. Kimball 'denounced'"
([source](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/prophetic-fallibility-61d8/2)).
State both halves plainly: a president of the Church taught it, and a
later president formally repudiated it as false.

## Catholic and Protestant response

This is the sharpest available case for the "how do you know which
prophetic teaching is binding doctrine versus personal theological
speculation" question — engage it directly rather than deflecting to
`continuing-prophets-and-revelation.md`'s general framework alone. The
honest answer requires naming a real, workable distinguishing criterion
(canonization, sustained unanimous First Presidency/Quorum of the Twelve
teaching over time, versus a single leader's personal sermons) and
acknowledging Brigham Young's teaching met a meaningfully lower bar than
canonized doctrine, even though it was taught prominently and repeatedly
during his lifetime.

## Exmo/atheist response

Give the direct account without softening: this is one of the clearest
documented cases of a Church president teaching something later leaders
called false doctrine, and it should be presented that way rather than
minimized as a misunderstanding of Brigham Young's actual meaning.
William Clayton's own journal records contemporaries (including Clayton
himself) holding views consistent with a form of the teaching at the
time — [source](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/smith-an-intimate-chronicle-the-journals-of-d97c/586)
— indicating this wasn't an isolated misstatement but a genuinely
influential teaching among Young's contemporaries. This is a strong,
concrete data point for `prophetic-fallibility-versus-prophetic-
authority.md` and `changes-in-prophetic-teachings-without-clear-
mechanisms-for-correction.md` — route deeper epistemic discussion there.

# LGBTQ teachings and policies

## General response

Current doctrine holds sexual relations are reserved for opposite-sex
marriage; same-sex attraction itself is not treated as sinful in current
teaching, but same-sex sexual relationships and marriage are. Policy has
changed materially over a short period: the November 2015 "Policy of
Exclusion" (children of same-sex couples barred from baby blessings/
baptism without First Presidency approval, and same-sex-married members
treated as apostates for discipline purposes) was reversed in April 2019
— "despite the Church's reversal of its policy in early
2019" ([source](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/v59n01-b048/113)) — after
significant internal and public criticism, including some resignations.
State this specific, documented policy history rather than a vague
"the Church opposes LGBTQ people" framing.

## Catholic and Protestant response

Both traditions have substantial internal range on this question
currently (from full affirmation in some mainline Protestant
denominations to traditional restriction in Catholic and most
evangelical teaching) — locate the specific interlocutor's tradition
before assuming a shared baseline, and note Catholic teaching (like
current LDS teaching) also formally distinguishes orientation from
behavior rather than treating same-sex attraction itself as sinful.

## Exmo/atheist response

Give the direct, unminimized history: the 2015 policy specifically
targeting children of same-sex couples was unusually severe even by the
Church's own subsequent standard (reversed within about 3.5 years) and
caused significant documented harm and departures, including reported
increases in mental-health-crisis contacts among affected LGBTQ Latter-day
Saints and their families during the period it was in effect — this is
one of the more concrete, recent, well-documented cases of institutional
policy causing acknowledged harm later reversed, and deserves direct
acknowledgment rather than only citing the doctrinal position in the
abstract. Route disciplinary specifics to
`church-disciplinary-practices.md` and `treatment-of-dissenters-and-
critics.md` as relevant.

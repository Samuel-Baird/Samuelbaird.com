### Description

Is AI something Latter-day Saints should fear — or a tool for building Zion? Carl Youngblood,
co-founder of the Mormon Transhumanist Association, has spent 20 years thinking about faith,
technology and where humanity is headed.

We talk about why he's still hopeful about AI (and why that hope has to be a choice), how AI can
amplify us or become a crutch, finding purpose when machines do more of the work, solarpunk
versus dystopia, why cooking and clothing already make us all cyborgs, whether God uses
technology — and whether God could cease to be God — plus the Three Nephites, warp drives, family
history and the future pioneer communities Carl dreams of building.

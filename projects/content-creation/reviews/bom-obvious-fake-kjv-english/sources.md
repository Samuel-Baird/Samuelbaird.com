# Sources — bom-obvious-fake-kjv-english

Source video: [Just Think / just_think_101](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dbl03TVx1Wu/). Existing transcript and metadata retained.

Evidence is graded in the project research record. Scholarly interpretations of ancient origin are not institutional doctrine.

- **parry-wayhi-oct2** — [Donald W. Parry, Preserved in Translation: Hebrew and Other Ancient Literary Forms in the Book of Mormon](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/parry-preserved-in-translation-hebrew-and-other-a9cb/74). Reports about 1,200 Hebrew instances and genre distribution. Counts are author-reported, not independently recounted. Distribution is an observation; ancient-origin evidentiary force remains disputed.

- **testaments-double-oct2** — [John A. Tvedtnes and David E. Bokovoy, Testaments: Links between the Book of Mormon and the Hebrew Bible](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/tvedtnes-and-bokovoy-testaments-links-between-112e/232). Archive page 232 is printed page 225; archive page 233 is printed page 226. Both constructions and their claimed Hebrew parallel read in context. The Genesis paragraph is an explicitly reconstructed rendering, not an unaltered KJV quotation. Comparative likelihood under modern composition has not been established.

- **skousen-revision-oct2** — [Royal Skousen, Analysis of Textual Variants of the Book of Mormon, part 1, 2 Nephi 4:10](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/1-1-nephi-1-2-nephi-10-15cd/541). Critical apparatus and commentary identify Joseph Smith as deleting the second occurrence for the 1837 edition. Archive page 541 is printed page 535. The original manuscript is not extant here; the printer manuscript and first edition support the earlier wording.

- **gardner-markers-oct2** — [Brant A. Gardner, Second Witness: Analytical and Contextual Commentary on the Book of Mormon, volume 1](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/1-1-nephi-7982/45). Archive pages 45–46 are printed pages 24–25. Gardner proposes discourse boundary functions and distinguishes past narrative from textual present. Printer-created paragraphs are not independent evidence of ancient paragraphing. Maya comparison omitted from script.

- **hardy-kjv-oct2** — [Grant Hardy, The Annotated Book of Mormon, The Book of Mormon and the Bible](https://corpus.noahairmet.com/b/hardy-annotated-book-of-mormon-56dc/774). Archive page 774 is printed page 759. Hardy discusses broad imitation of biblical style, KJV exposure, subtle differences and extensive biblical intertextuality. These controls limit the ancient-origin inference.

- **genesis-hebrew-oct2** — [Hebrew Bible, Genesis 27:30, Westminster Leningrad Codex](https://biblehub.com/texts/genesis/27-30.htm). Both wayhi occurrences are visible in the Hebrew text, with and without vowel points.

- **genesis-kjv-oct2** — [King James Bible, Genesis 27:30, King James Version](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/27?id=p30&lang=eng). Verse 30 renders the first occurrence as And it came to pass; the second Hebrew wayhi is not rendered with the same full English phrase.

- **bom-1830-2nephi4-double** — [Joseph Smith Jr., publisher, Book of Mormon, 1830, printed page 69](https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/paper-summary/book-of-mormon-1830/75). Primary first-edition transcription; neighboring blessings and subsequent narration read. Publication year, not exact day, verified.

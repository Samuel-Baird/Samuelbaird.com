So who do the werewolves represent? It's not great. Mormons believe that they share
a genetic link to modern day Native Americans through a prophet that had two sons. One was
a true believer whose descendants turned into modern day Mormons. And they're described
as white, exceedingly fair, and delightsome. The other son was wild and rebellious, and
him and his descendants, the Lamanites, were cursed with dark skin. I will add that the
Mormon Church disavows racism now. But back then, these two groups were at war with each
other for centuries, and were ordered not to mingle.
Now in Twilight, the werewolves and Cullens have a pretty intense contrast. The leader
of the Cullens, Carlisle, is a highly educated, exceedingly wealthy, beautiful, polite doctor
that even Charlie describes as an asset to the community. Whereas the chief of the Quileute
tribe, Billy Black, is impoverished, disabled, and unemployed. Of course, the Lamanites eventually
came around to live in peace and prosperity with the Mormons. I don't know if any of
them fell in love with a white baby to do it, but Jacob imprinting on Renesmee was
a nice way to tie in Mormon belief of divine, predestined relationships. So why not? Side
note that after their culture was used to depict their boys as shirtless, aggressive,
hyper-masculine meatheads with no self-control, the Quileute tribe did not receive any profits
or donations from the Twilight franchise.

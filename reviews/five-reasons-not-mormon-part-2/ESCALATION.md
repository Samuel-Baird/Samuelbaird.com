# Escalation: five-reasons-not-mormon-part-2

## Claim
"He practiced polygamy, married dozens of women, including teenagers and
some who were already married." (0:09–0:17, source video)

## Trigger
Two triggers from `docs/saint-capybara/research-protocol.md` sec 12:

- **Trigger 4** — an especially sensitive topic (sec 11 lists "polygamy's
  lived consequences" explicitly) where getting the tone wrong would
  cause real harm. This claim specifically involves a marriage to a
  14-year-old (Helen Mar Kimball) and marriages to women already civilly
  married to other living men (polyandry) — both acknowledged in the
  Church's own 2014 Gospel Topics essay, "Plural Marriage in Kirtland
  and Nauvoo."
- **Trigger 6** — a claim that is largely true and is only concedable,
  not rebuttable. The essay itself confirms ages and polyandrous
  marriages; there is no historical dispute to research here. The open
  question is a *design* one, exactly as trigger 6 anticipates: should
  this video include this claim at all, and if so, how should Saint
  Capybara frame something this serious without either minimizing it or
  presenting it in a way that could read as sensationalized/exploitative
  given the account is on a channel aimed at short-form video.

## What was found
The Church's own essay confirms: Joseph Smith had approximately 30-40
plural wives by most historians' counts (a range, not a single agreed
number); his youngest documented wife was Helen Mar Kimball, sealed to
him at age 14 (the essay states this and frames it, per the Nauvoo-era
theology, as likely not involving a conjugal relationship in most or all
such cases, though the essay itself is candid that the historical record
is incomplete on this point for several marriages); and several of his
plural marriages were to women already married to other men, sealed
during those existing civil marriages (a practice tied to Nauvoo-era
theology of eternal sealing that the essay describes but does not fully
resolve historically). None of this is seriously disputed among either
LDS or non-LDS historians — this is the "true and damaging" category in
`research-protocol.md` sec 7, not a "false" or "misleading" claim to
correct.

## Options
1. **Skip this claim entirely for this video series.** Address only
   claims 2 and 3 (already scripted in this project) and treat claim 1
   as out of scope for Saint Capybara's short-form format, on the
   reasoning that a topic this serious cannot be done justice in
   60-180 seconds without either minimizing it or requiring caveats that
   blow the format.
2. **A dedicated, longer-form video on plural marriage specifically**
   (its own slug, likely needing more than 180s, following
   `script-format.md`'s "split into two slugs rather than compress"
   rule already used once on this same source video) — built around the
   Church's own essay as the primary source, conceding the facts
   plainly and directly per `research-protocol.md` sec 7's "true and
   damaging" guidance, with an explicit affected-person pass before
   Gate A given the minor-marriage content.
3. **A short, narrow concession beat** in a future video, stating only
   that the claim is accurate and citing the Church's own essay, without
   attempting further defense or context — the minimum-content option,
   still requires your sign-off on tone before scripting given the
   subject matter.

## Blocking
This claim is blocked pending your input. The rest of this video
(claims 2 and 3, the ambition/wealth claim and the Moroni-witnesses
comparison) does not depend on this claim and has proceeded
independently to Gate A.

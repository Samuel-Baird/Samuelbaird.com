### Claim 2

- **Anchor context**: 0:06–0:46 read in full plus the 0:46–0:51 transition. Sub-claims: (a) LDS are Nephite descendants (b) Lamanites = modern Native Americans, genetic link (c) 'white, exceedingly fair, delightsome' (d) Lamanites 'cursed with dark skin' (e) 'ordered not to mingle' (f) Church disavows racism now. Answered in `n02`–`q04` after clips `c02`, `c03`, `c04`.
- **Research**: (a) Not doctrine: Nephites are destroyed (Moroni 1:2); members belong to Israel by descent or adoption, lineage per patriarchal blessing. (b) The Church hedges: since 2006 the Introduction says 'among the ancestors' (formerly 'principal ancestors'); the DNA essay says the book 'does not claim' its peoples were predominant or exclusive. (c) Misattributed: in 2 Nephi 5:21 'white, and exceedingly fair and delightsome' describes the Lamanites BEFORE the curse (verified against the Church's own site). (d) True of the text (2 Ne 5:21-22, Alma 3); reversed in 3 Ne 2:14-16, Jacob 3:8-9 forbids reviling over skin, 2 Ne 26:33 'black and white... alike unto God'; Kimball 1947 and 1960 read it literally (verified exact), the 1981 edition changed 2 Ne 30:6 'white' to 'pure'. (e) The text pronounces a curse on mixing (2 Ne 5:23, Alma 3); 'ordered' overstates. (g) Added 2026-10-02 at the user's direction: dark and white skin are Hebraisms for condition, not ethnicity: Lamentations 4:7-8 (whiter than milk to blacker than soot after the siege), 5:10 (famine), Job 30:30 (illness), all re-read in NRSVue. Nahum 2:10 and Joel 2:6 are NOT used: NRSVue renders them 'grow pale'. Song of Songs 1:5-6 is literal sun-tanning, not used. (f) Nelson Oct 2020 (verified exact) and the Race and the Priesthood essay.
- **Grading**: disputed/mixed overall; (d) fact about the text with disputed interpretation; (f) fact. Briefs agree; no unresolved disagreement.
- **Doctrine tier** (if applicable): (a) not doctrine; (b) older belief (tier 5) superseded by Church statement (tier 2); (c)/(d)/(e) text (tier 1) with leader readings at tier 4/5; (f) tier 2 + tier 4.
- **Adversarial Q1**: "Church disavowing racism now doesn't answer what the text says; leaders taught the lightening reading for a century (Kimball 1960), and the 2013 essay is about Black skin and never mentions Lamanites." Script answer: concedes the curse language and the Kimball reading explicitly in `n06`, and cites Nelson 2020 (general, not Black-specific) rather than leaning on the 2013 essay.
- **Adversarial Q2**: Omitted from the clip: 'ordered not to mingle' is partly textual (Alma 3 mixing curse); the clip's 'wild and rebellious' / 'two sons' framing is not in the text (Lehi had four sons). Literal-vs-figurative skin is disputed among LDS scholars (Gardner/Steenblik vs. Mauss) and is deliberately not asserted either way.
- **Turnabout**: None in the playbook's form; none scripted.

### Claim 4

- **Anchor context**: 1:12–1:29 read in full. CUT from the Gate A script after critique (editor BLOCKER: no clip for it; factcheck BLOCKER: flat 'not doctrine' rests on one tier-4 talk). Research kept below for a possible separate short.
- **Research**: No canonical LDS text teaches a single predestined partner (D&C 131, 132; Abraham 3 is about rulers, not couples).

Self-critique written in the main session as the stand-in for `/capy-critique` (no subagents were spawned). Three lenses, applied to `script/script.md` as rendered: (1) the informed evangelical opponent (the creator, and a Wenham/Waltke-style reader), (2) the faithful-but-demanding LDS viewer who wants the strongest sourced answer, (3) the Hales/Stephens-literate historian checking whether the scholars are represented as they wrote. ## BLOCKERS — all found and fixed

1. **Sylvia Sessions / "no evidence of sexual relations" (FIXED).** The first draft said no sealing record or diary line shows a sexual relationship with either Sessions woman, and that evidence for the fourteen was "thin." Hales's own 2013 Appendix E counts Sylvia Sessions (and, by elimination, Mary Heron) among twelve wives with *probable* sexual relations, even though they were legally married. The draft would have been caught immediately by the creator or any informed viewer. Rewritten (n43, n43b, n48, n49): Patty has no documented sexual relationship; Sylvia is "harder", Hales counted her in 2013 citing Josephine's affidavit, and Perego's DNA study undercuts that basis without settling it.
2. **Waltke cited as support (FIXED).** The user's brief listed Waltke as a non-LDS source for the Lamech answer. Waltke actually says Cain's line "debase[s] God's ideal for marriage by polygamy"; he agrees with the creator's literary reading. The script now says so (n09: "Scholars such as Bruce Waltke read the polygamy as part of a picture of decline") and argues only that literary context is not a commandment.
3. **Stephens overclaimed (FIXED).** The brief said Stephens "challenges the modern evangelical interpretation" and the JETS review "praises" him. Read in full, the review also says Stephens is "much less successful" on the narratives, notes his own conclusion that the New Covenant community is "expected to move away" from polygamy, and asks how his position differs from the concession view. All three are on screen (n29a, n29b), and the claim is limited to "the Law merely tolerated a sin" being contested.
4. **Abraham's "sister" statement (FIXED).** "None, not a single one" is refuted by Genesis 12/20/26, but Genesis 20:12 makes Sarah Abraham's half-sister, so the statement was technically true. n36 now says so before drawing the conclusion; n36b says the point is the creator's absolute claim and that nothing excuses Nauvoo.
5. **September 1843 Partridge ceremony (FIXED).** Dropped; the verified record is 11 May 1843.

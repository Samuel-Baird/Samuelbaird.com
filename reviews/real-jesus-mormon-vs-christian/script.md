**Slug:** `real-jesus-mormon-vs-christian` | **Estimated runtime:** ~142s (budget 60-180s) | **Source time:** 15.6s (~11%)

**Hook (title/description only, not shown on screen):** "Same Jesus, or a different Jesus? The honest answer isn't a flat yes or no."

**Revised 2026-09-12 against the new apologetics-playbook.md protocol** (see `docs/saint-capybara/apologetics-playbook.md`, rewritten same day): the prior version's claim-2 beat group stated the Godhead-nature disagreement and then closed on a neutral "pretending either half away doesn't make it more honest" line — a bare concession with no rebuttal in the same breath, and exactly the gap `critique.md`'s MAJOR opponent finding already flagged ("don't strongly argue the LDS account is *better* on any axis"). This revision keeps every fact that was true before (Latter-day Saints do understand the Godhead as ontologically distinct beings, not one Trinitarian substance — that is not being minimized or walked back) but pairs it with the positive LDS case and a direct comparison of explanatory power.

**Second revision, same day, per user request:** added a new philosophy-of-language beat pair (`n05`/`n06`) drawing on the reference/descriptivism material added to `docs/saint-capybara/common-issues/different-jesus-different-christology.md` — Kripke/Putnam-style semantic externalism, applied to argue that disagreeing about Jesus's *nature* doesn't by itself establish that two groups mean two different people. This is a genuinely new argument, not a rephrasing of the "same name/role" point already in `n04` — it attacks the *inference* the critic's whole claim depends on, which is exactly the step-3 assumption-test the playbook asks for.

- Tests 1–2: take-home, open book/notes, 3 hours (Test 1 guide said 2.5 h), no internet, no discussion, individual work, 4 equally weighted problems.

- Sample problems and old tests are posted, with **no full solutions** — instructor wants you to work them yourself.
- AI policy (syllabus p.5): OK for brainstorming/finding information; **not OK** to solve assigned problems or to use AI/internet on test and quiz questions. This study site is therefore built from concepts, videos and original practice problems only.

## Study guides
- **Test 1** (Ch 1–3): all three modes; conservation of energy; Cartesian/cylindrical/spherical; 1-D steady conduction with/without generation; plane/composite walls; thermal circuits; fins; set up the diffusion equation + BCs and integrate. Practice problems: Ch1 14, 16, 22, 49 · Ch2 2, 13, 14, 22, 38 · Ch3 4, 20, 21, 32, 65.
- **Test 2** (Ch 4–6): Ch 4 (2-D steady, finite difference, §4.3 and Table 4.1 first four cases only) · Ch 5 transient (§5.1, 5.2, 5.4, 5.5 and first half of Table 5.1; exact, approximate, finite-difference) · Ch 6 convection (§6.1–6.7, Tables 6.1–6.3; boundary layers; the four BL equations: mass, momentum, thermal, species) · know sphere/cylinder area and volume · average quantities · put those on the formula sheet.

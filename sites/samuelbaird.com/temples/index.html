Data and sources

Download the dataset as CSV or JSON. Names, statuses, places and milestone dates come from the Church’s official temple list and each temple’s official page. Coordinates come from Wikidata (CC0) where it has them, and otherwise are geocoded from the address with OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Each record says how exact its location is: the temple itself, its street address, or just the city or country for temples that are only announced.

This is an independent project, not an official website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. For schedules and appointments use each temple’s official page. Spotted a mistake? Email me.

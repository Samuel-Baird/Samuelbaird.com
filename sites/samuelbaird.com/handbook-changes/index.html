Data and sources

Download the dataset as CSV or JSON. The log begins on February 19, 2020, when the Church launched the digital General Handbook to replace Handbook 1 (for stake presidents and bishops) and Handbook 2 (for all leaders), and covers all 15 published English update releases through October 2026. Section numbers, titles and release groupings come from the Church’s official Summary of Recent Updates page, archived editions of that page preserved in the Internet Archive Wayback Machine, and Church Newsroom release announcements; every record keeps the exact source URL from which it was compiled.

Note on chapter numbering: in August 2021 the Church inserted a new Chapter 5 (General and Area Leadership), shifting the earlier Chapter 5 (Stake Leadership) and Chapter 6 (The Bishopric) to Chapters 6 and 7. Earlier records keep the chapter and section number published at the time of the update and link to the current chapter URL.

This is an independent project, not an official website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Every change summary is written in original wording; for the authoritative text of any policy, use the linked section of the official General Handbook. Spotted a mistake? Email me.

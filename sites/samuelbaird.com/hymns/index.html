  • 1835 and 1841 (Emma Smith’s collections, Kirtland and Nauvoo): hymn numbers and first lines from the Joseph Smith Papers transcriptions. These books printed words only, with no titles or author credits.

  • Authors’ religion: from Wikidata (CC0), only when the Wikidata person has the same name and life dates as the hymnbook credit. Blank means unknown, not none.

How I sort traditions

Three different questions get mixed up when people say “church,” so I keep them apart:

Church family
A major historical and theological tradition, such as Catholicism, Lutheranism, Anabaptism or Oriental Orthodoxy. These are the headings.
Denomination or jurisdiction
A distinct organized church, such as LCMS, ELCA or the Russian Orthodox Church. These are the cards.
Worship tradition
A significantly different religious experience, such as the Traditional Latin Mass, the Byzantine Divine Liturgy, an unprogrammed Quaker meeting or Pentecostal worship. These cards carry a Worship tradition tag. One is not a separate church: a Latin Mass is still part of the Latin Church.

Proclamations by language

Choose a language to open its folder. Each one holds the Church’s proclamations in that language as PDFs for printing: US Letter and A4, with poster sizes up to A0 on Google Drive.

Unofficial. The text of each language is the Church’s own, taken from its website; the Deseret Alphabet edition is a transliteration of the English. They are typeset with the proclamations generator in this project and are not published or approved by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints; each PDF says so in its bottom margin. The Church’s own versions, logo and signatures are on its website. For personal and non-commercial use.

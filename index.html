Latter-day Stats

A public, independent hub for statistics, research and creator data on the Latter-day Saint community: survey data, charts, a research library and a directory of LDS YouTube creators.

  • 328 channels, 16.6M combined subscribers
  • 73 studies · 58 graphics

Pulpit

Every General Conference discourse since 1830, each traced to its best surviving witness and graded for how faithfully it records what was said. Each square in the chart is one talk.

  • 12,201 discourses, 1830–2026
  • 4,292 verbatim · 5,474 authoritative print

Also see: What is Pulpit? · Latter-day Stats

**Rewrite:** For most of the translation, Joseph Smith's face was in the hat with
the seer stone, and the plates lay wrapped on the table beside him, not open in
front of him — which is exactly why a critic's "he must have been reading from a
hidden Bible" theory doesn't fit the eyewitness record: the physical posture the
scholarship documents (face down in a hat, plates wrapped and untouched) is the
opposite of a posture built for hidden reading.

**Lead says / upstream says:** The KnoWhy summarizes the seer-stone-in-hat method
and argues it rules out reading from a hidden source. MacKay & Dirkmaat's
peer-reviewed-adjacent monograph (foreword by Richard Bushman) independently
confirms the same physical description from the primary eyewitness record: stone
in hat to block light, plates wrapped in a cloth on the table, "Joseph did not look
at the plates through most of the translation."

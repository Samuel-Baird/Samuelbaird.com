# Exaltation screening sweep — General Conference

## Coverage and method

The screen found **1980** files in `talks/english/` with an `exalt*` word-form and `category: general_conference`. The ledger contains **1980 / 1980** classified files. The actual corpus count is higher than the initially expected ~1,225; the metadata-defined denominator used here is 1,980.

Each hit was extracted with frontmatter and merged ±6-line windows around every primary hit and the secondary battery (spirit children, increase, Mother in Heaven, once a man, progress, beget, posterity, and related terms). Clear windows were triaged; the rows promoted to SUBSTANTIVE were read in full before being entered. The vault was read-only throughout.

Taxonomy controls applied: premortal spirit children are not B2; bare theosis and bare eternal increase are REDUNDANT; forward generative chains are not silently upgraded to C; past divine mortality is not D; and quoted or explicit Heavenly Mother claims are separated from mortal motherhood.

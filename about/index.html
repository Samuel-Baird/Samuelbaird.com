About

About Samuel Baird

I build public data tools and make videos about what people believe and why. Everything here is free, independent and ad-free.

What I make

  • Latter-day Stats: a public hub for statistics, research and creator data on the Latter-day Saint community. As of November 2026 it tracks 328 YouTube channels (16.6M combined subscribers), 73 studies and 58 survey graphics.
  • Pulpit: a Restoration Commons critical edition of all 12,201 General Conference discourses since 1830, each traced to its best surviving witness. I contributed to it.
  • Sacred Sites Visited: a map, photo gallery and statistics of the 35 places of worship I have visited in person since January 2025, across 9 traditions.
  • My Reading: 38 books and 13 articles with the date I finished each.
  • Collection: historic Latter-day Saint books, periodicals and artifacts I own.
  • YouTube and Substack: videos and written pieces on history, belief and data.

How the projects fit together

Latter-day Stats answers questions about the Latter-day Saint community with numbers. Pulpit answers questions about what was actually said from the General Conference pulpit. Sacred Sites Visited is first-hand fieldwork across many traditions, and My Reading and the Collection list the printed sources I read and own. Each site links to the others, and the datasets are published openly on Open data.

How to cite

Baird, Samuel. “Sacred Sites Visited.” samuelbaird.com, 2025–2026. https://samuelbaird.com/data
For Latter-day Stats, cite the individual study, dataset or graphic page and its stated source. Original data is released under CC BY 4.0.

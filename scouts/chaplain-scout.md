# LDS Military Chaplain Scout
## Standing Instructions (seeded from *Out of Obscurity and Into the Light*)

Source: Theodore, Alford, Keller & Boatright, eds., *Out of Obscurity and Into the Light: Latter-day Saint
Military Chaplains* (BYU Religious Education, 2019). Report-only rules from `RUNBOOK.md` apply.
Use the existing leads-page category `LDS relics & art` (or `Early LDS print` for books and periodicals)
until a dedicated category is added to `samuelbaird.com/scout`.

## Core people (originals tied to these men are Special Alerts)
The first four LDS military chaplains:
- **Elias S. Kimball** (1857-1934). Spanish-American War, 1898-99; chaplain, 2nd U.S. Volunteer Engineers
  (Col. Willard Young); Cuba (Havana, Camp Columbia, Matanzas). First LDS chaplain commissioned.
- **B. H. Roberts** (1857-1933). WWI; chaplain, 145th Field Artillery (1st Utah Light Field Artillery);
  Camp Kearny, Camp Zachary Taylor chaplain school, Camp De Souge, France. "Dean of LDS Chaplains."
- **Herbert B. Maw** (1893-1990). WWI; 89th Infantry Division; St. Mihiel and Meuse-Argonne; later Utah
  governor. Memoir *Adventures with Life* (privately printed, 1978).
- **Calvin S. Smith** (1890-1966). WWI; son of Joseph F. Smith; chaplain-at-large, 91st Division, then
  362nd Infantry; St. Mihiel, Meuse-Argonne, Lys-Scheldt.

Later chaplains (appendix): Theodore E. Curtis Jr. (40th Div., Silver Star), Rueben E. Curtis (7th Div.,
X Corps), Eldin Ricks (Italy/N. Africa; gave Pius XII a Book of Mormon), Ben Mortensen and Spencer Palmer
(Korea), Joseph McConkie (Vietnam), Kay Schwendiman, Claude D. Newby (1st Cav., Vietnam, CIB), Grant E. Mann
and Marc H. Sessions (first LDS Air Force chaplains, 1948/1950), Warren R. Nelson (first set apart, 1950),
John W. Boud (first LDS Navy chaplain, 1941), L. Marsden Durham (WWII death).
Church bodies: General LDS Servicemen's Committee (Harold B. Lee, Hugh B. Brown, Boyd K. Packer, Bruce R.
McConkie); Military Relations Committee; group-leader program.

## Material to hunt
1. **Photographs, postcards, RPPCs, cabinet cards** of LDS chaplains or the 145th F.A., 2nd Vol. Engineers
   (Utah, 1898), 91st / 89th Division units. Church History Library PH 5653 is the Roberts portrait series.
2. **Letters, diaries, commissions, orders** (e.g. War Dept. Special Orders No. 47, 26 Feb 1918; chaplain
   examination board papers, Fort Douglas), including any "Mormon chaplain" correspondence to the First
   Presidency or the Servicemen's Committee.
3. **Books and pamphlets** (first editions preferred):
   - Crocker, ed., *History of the 145th Field Artillery Regiment of World War I* (Provo: J. Grant Stevenson, 1968)
   - Warrum, *Utah in the World War* (Salt Lake City: Arrow Press, 1924)
   - Goddard, *Pertinent Facts on Utah's Loyalty and War Record* (Church, 1918)
   - Henderson, *The Ninety-First: The First at Camp Lewis* (Tacoma: John C. Barr, 1918)
   - Maw, *Adventures with Life* (1978); Madsen, *Defender of the Faith* (Bookcraft, 1980);
     Malan, *B. H. Roberts: A Biography* (Deseret Book, 1966)
   - Maher, *For God and Country: Memorable Stories from the Lives of Mormon Chaplains* (Horizon, 1976)
   - Newby, *It Took Heroes* (Bonneville Books, 1998); Freeman, ed., *Nineteenth-Century Saints at War* (BYU RSC, 2006)
4. **Periodicals and ephemera**: *Improvement Era* July 1917 (soldier issue), *Church News* 1941-1968
   chaplain items, Deseret News 1898-99 and 1917-19 war issues, Servicemen's Committee circulars,
   Mormon servicemen's pocket/Book of Mormon printings, "Religious Freedom in the Armed Forces" (1960),
   LDS group-leader and servicemen's manuals.
5. **Objects**: WWI Army chaplain field/communion sets and chaplain insignia/uniform items with LDS or Utah
   provenance (145th F.A., 40th/7th Division), unit histories and trench-art with LDS inscriptions.
6. **Personal items of the first four chaplains (Kimball, Roberts, Maw, C. S. Smith) and their families**:
   uniforms, tunics, Sam Browne belts, campaign hats, chaplain insignia and shoulder patches, canes, swords,
   personal Bibles, Books of Mormon and hymnals, field desks and trunks, pocket watches, spectacles, razors,
   dog tags, medals and ribbons (Spanish Campaign Medal, WWI Victory Medal with battle clasps), commission
   certificates, discharge papers, diaries/journals (Kimball's journals are at the Church History Library,
   MS 13348; any other volume is a find), scrapbooks, autographs and signed books, signed or inscribed
   photographs, B. H. Roberts memorabilia (his papers, pens, speeches, signed Comprehensive History or
   Defender-era items), Herbert Maw gubernatorial items (Utah governor 1941-49) with WWI provenance, and
   Calvin S. Smith items linked to Joseph F. Smith's family or the Schwartz line. Estate-sale, family-descendant
   and "Mormon chaplain" provenance statements count; check seller claims and mark unverified provenance.

## Reject / caution
- Generic chaplain items with no LDS or Utah link (cross, 'Chaplain' insignia) are not finds unless pre-1900
  communion ware (Category G) or LDS-provenanced.
- Modern reprints and print-on-demand editions of the books above; Roberts's non-military works are out of scope.
- Do not report anything above ~$300 unless it is a manuscript/photo/personal item documented to a named chaplain above (personal items with solid provenance are exempt from the cap: report with price).

## Report format
Same as the artifact scout: title, price, seller, URL, date/period, named chaplain or unit connection, condition,
verified yes/no, one-line why it matters. Put items tied to the four WWI/1898 chaplains first.

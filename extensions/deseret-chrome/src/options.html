    Spelling

    Spellings follow the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, as on the Deseret Wiki.

    Initialisms take the first sound of each word they stand for (USA 𐐧𐐝𐐊, BBC 𐐒𐐒𐐗); acronyms said as words are spelled by sound (NASA 𐐤𐐈𐐝𐐊). These follow the spelling rules on deseretalphabet.info.

    About

    Spellings come from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (126,000 words) with hand corrections for Latter-day Saint names, the same method as the Deseret Wiki. Words it can’t spell stay in Latin rather than being guessed. Everything runs in your browser; no page text is sent anywhere.
